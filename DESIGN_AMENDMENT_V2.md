## Usability contract
Plain-language instructions; clear next action; editable preview controls with labels/focus; simpler navigation for members/applicants; explicit confirmations and safe recovery; explained no-activity reporting; no secret display; separate operator and restricted case views. Final real-user accessibility, language review and usability testing remain stage acceptance obligations.
